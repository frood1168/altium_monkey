• why have both U500 and U501?

The I2C interface (U500) is the default mux control interface from the HackRF. The GPIO controlled interface (U502) is for "FAST" antenna switching – driven by GPIO.

• Did you ever check with the customer to see if he needs the logic to change the antenna channels?

In the last meeting we had with him, we discussed keeping it the same. In the document he sent last week, he does about keeping things so that the applications work as they are now. It's a low-impact section, with stuffing options that will accommodate whatever the customer ends up wanting. The mux logic for the opera cake is decoded with the logic gates from U502-U508 to select the correct channel on the new mux chip, and they can be DNI'd with the right 0ohm jumpers.
